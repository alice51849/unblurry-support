What the app does

Reduce blur, enhance detail, compare the preview and save the result while keeping the original photo safe.

Troubleshooting

Close and reopen the app, confirm that the latest App Store version is installed, and include the device model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce when emailing support.

Data and privacy

Selected photos are processed on the device with Apple imaging frameworks and the bundled enhancement model. Unblurry does not upload your photos to a developer server. No account is required. The app contains no third-party advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics.

Purchase

Previewing is available before purchase. Free saves use the disclosed quality limits; an optional one-time purchase unlocks full-quality output and Apple handles the purchase.

Deletion and recovery

Delete records from inside the app when that control is available. Deleting the app removes its local container; Apple-managed backups remain governed by your device settings.

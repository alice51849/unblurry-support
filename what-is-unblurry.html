What is Unblurry?

Unblurry is an iPhone photo enhancer that turns soft, shaky and out-of-focus shots into sharper, more vibrant photos in seconds. It all happens on the iPhone: no cloud upload and no account. It has eight modes: Auto Clear for everyday blur, Sharpen for soft focus and camera shake, Denoise for grain and high-ISO noise, Low Light for dark shots, Document for text, scans and whiteboards, AI Super Resolution (a neural model that rebuilds detail on the device), 4× Upscale, and Portrait & Restore for faces and old photos. A before/after slider lets you press to see the original.

Who is Unblurry for?

People with photos that came out blurry or shaky, dark shots taken in low light, grainy pictures, photos of text, scans or whiteboards that need to be readable, and old photos or portraits worth reviving. It also suits people who would rather their photos never leave the phone.

How does Unblurry charge?

Free download with an in-app purchase. You can enhance and save for free every day. A single one-time purchase of Unblurry Pro removes watermarks, exports at full original resolution, and adds 4× upscaling, the Portrait & Restore modes, unlimited daily saves and all premium themes. There is no subscription. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is Unblurry different from other photo apps?

Based on what the listing describes: processing is 100% on the device, so photos are not uploaded; there are eight modes for different problems rather than a single filter; an instant before/after slider shows the original at a press; results are tuned to look natural and protect true color; and it is a one-time purchase with no subscription, no ads and no tracking.

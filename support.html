Getting started

  • Pick a photo: tap New Photo to open a blurry or low‑quality image from your library.
  • Choose a mode: tap a mode in the rail — AI Clarity, Auto Clear, Sharpen, Denoise, Low Light, Document, Upscale, Portrait or Restore.
  • Compare: press and hold the photo to peek at the original, release to see the result. Pinch to zoom in on the detail.

AI super resolution

The AI Clarity mode runs a neural model that rebuilds real detail and edges. It runs entirely on your device — no network, and nothing leaves your phone. Larger photos take a little longer; keep the app open while it works.

Modes & strength

Use the strength slider to dial each effect in to taste. Auto Clear balances sharpening, denoise and contrast automatically; the other modes target a specific problem such as low light, noise, small size or an old print.

Saving & sharing

Tap Save to render your photo to your library, or Share to send it to Messages, Instagram and more. Free results are watermarked and resolution‑capped; the one‑time unlock saves at full resolution with no watermark.

Free trial & unlock

Enhancing and previewing are free, and AI Clarity includes a few free trials so you can see the result on your own photos. The one‑time unlock removes the watermark and opens full‑resolution exports, 4× upscaling, Portrait & Restore modes, unlimited saves and every theme. It’s a single purchase — no subscription. Restore it any time from the unlock screen.

Photos don’t appear?

Check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Unblurry and make sure access is allowed.

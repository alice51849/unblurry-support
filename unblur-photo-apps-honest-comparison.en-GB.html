Unblurry vs other photo-unblur apps on iPhone: what you pay and what you get

If you’re looking for an app to fix a blurry or shaky photo, most of the popular options are free to download and then ask for a weekly or yearly subscription. On iPhone, Unblurry’s only paid option is a single one-time in-app purchase. That isn’t automatically the right choice for everyone, so here is a side-by-side look. We make Unblurry, so to keep this fair we’ve only used what each app’s UK App Store page says (checked 26 November 2026).

Where Unblurry is a good fit

  • You want to pay once on iPhone rather than keep a subscription running.
  • You’d rather your photos aren’t uploaded: the App Store page says processing happens on your device, with no account needed.
  • You mainly need to rescue soft focus, camera shake, grain, dark shots or a blurry document.

Where another app may suit you better (things Unblurry doesn’t do)

  • Colourising black & white photos, animating photos, scratch removal, object or background removal, AI portraits or cartoon effects — these appear on the pages of Pixelup, PhotoApp or Unblur, but not in Unblurry’s description.
  • The free version has limits: exports carry a watermark and are not at full original resolution; 4× upscaling and the Portrait & Restore mode need Unblurry Pro.
  • Requires iOS 17 or later, so older iPhones can’t install it.
  • No UK ratings yet on the App Store, so you have fewer other people’s opinions to go on than with the established apps above.
  • On-device processing depends on your iPhone; we haven’t published speed comparisons, so we make no claims about speed or result quality versus the apps above.

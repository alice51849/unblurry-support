How do I unblur a photo on iPhone — and which mode should I pick?

Unblurry turns soft, shaky and out-of-focus shots into sharp, vibrant photos in seconds, and it all happens right on your iPhone. The trick is matching the mode to the kind of blur.

  1. Open the blurry photo in Unblurry.
  2. Everyday blur: start with Auto Clear — one tap.
  3. Soft focus or camera shake: use Sharpen. Grainy, high-ISO shots: use Denoise. Dark shots: use Low Light.
  4. Text, scans or whiteboards: use Document. Need more detail or a bigger image: try AI Super Resolution or 4× Upscale; for faces and old photos, Portrait & Restore.
  5. Drag the before/after slider (press to peek at the original), then save the version you like.

100% on-device — your photos never leave your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
